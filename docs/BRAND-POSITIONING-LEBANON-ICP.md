# Brand Positioning + Lebanon ICP

## Brand Name

- Arabic: **سوق الشام**
- English: **SoukSham**
- Domain: **souksham.shop**
- Market: **Lebanon only for this build**

## Strategic Positioning

SoukSham is a premium Lebanese Arabic DTC store that curates practical products and presents them with clarity, authority, and trust.

> "منتجات مختارة بعناية لنهارك بلبنان — واضحة، مجرّبة، وبطلب آمن مع الدفع عند الاستلام."

The brand should feel like it owns and curates the product line, even if fulfillment is dropship/agent-based. The site must never say "dropshipping".

## ICP

Primary ICP: Lebanese buyers, Arabic-first, mobile-first, influenced by TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Snapchat-style UGC.

Women can be a key buyer segment, but the site should not sound Saudi or Gulf. It should sound Lebanese-friendly and trustworthy across Beirut, Mount Lebanon, North, South, and Bekaa.

### Psychological Triggers

- Wants convenience without getting scammed.
- Skeptical of cheap-looking pages and unrealistic ads.
- Trusts stores that show real details, policies, COD, and phone confirmation.
- Responds to emotional pain: embarrassment, wasted time, heat, home/family comfort, "I deserve something that works".
- Needs low-risk buying: COD, clear phone confirmation, delivery expectation, exchange policy.
- Likes premium when it feels justified: "مرتب، واضح، نوعية، بيستاهل".

### Lebanon-Specific Trust Requirements

- COD clearly visible.
- Prices in USD unless business later decides otherwise.
- Lebanese mobile validation.
- Delivery areas and expectation.
- Real support email.
- Clear exchange/returns page.
- Product proof: material, use cases, comparison, FAQs.
- UGC that feels local: Lebanese names/areas only when real.

### Lebanese Arabic Tone

Use a clean Lebanese-friendly Arabic, not overly slangy and not Gulf.

Good phrases:

- "بيوصلك"
- "بتطلبي"
- "رقم موبايلك"
- "الدفع عند الاستلام"
- "بلا دفع مسبق"
- "منأكد الطلب قبل التجهيز"
- "واضح، مرتب، وبيستاهل"
- "مناسب لنهارك بلبنان"
- "زحمة، شمس، مشاوير، ووقت قليل"

Avoid:

- Saudi/Gulf forms like "تقدرين", "جوالك", "يوصل لك", "أضيفيه" unless a campaign specifically targets Gulf.
- Over-heavy Lebanese slang that feels unprofessional.
- Claims like "الأول في لبنان" unless proven.

## Brand Promise

> "منختار لك منتجات عملية بتستاهل سعرها، منشرحها بوضوح، ومنخلي الطلب سهل وآمن بالدفع عند الاستلام."

## Premium Price Justification

High price is justified by:

- Branded presentation.
- Better bundles and curated offers.
- Clear use cases for Lebanon.
- Product specs and proof.
- COD and confirmation.
- Exchange guarantee.
- Professional support.

Premium does not come from fake certificates or fake reviews.

## Authority Stack

Use these layers:

1. **Curation authority:** "مختار ضمن معايير سوق الشام".
2. **Mechanism authority:** explain how product works in plain language.
3. **Material/source proof:** supplier certificate/photo slots, product specs.
4. **Social proof:** UGC, ratings, order count, customer photos once real.
5. **Operational proof:** COD, phone confirmation, policies, delivery expectations.
6. **Brand consistency:** logo, color, typography, packaging mockups.

Do not invent certificates. Use copy like:

- "صور وشهادات المورد تُضاف هنا بعد التحقق."
- "تقييمات عملاء حقيقية تُعرض بعد أول دفعة طلبات."
- "مختار وفق معايير سوق الشام: الفائدة، الوضوح، قابلية الاستخدام، وقيمة العرض."

## Home Page Positioning

The home page must position SoukSham as a trusted Lebanese curated store:

1. Hero: brand promise + trust badges + CTA to collection.
2. "ليش سوق الشام؟" curation story.
3. Featured collection/cards.
4. Proof framework: how products are selected.
5. COD trust and delivery reassurance.
6. UGC/social proof preview slots.
7. FAQ.

## About Page Positioning

About page should explain:

- SoukSham exists because buyers in Lebanon are tired of unclear pages and random products.
- The brand selects products by usefulness, proof, customer fit, and clear offers.
- COD-first because trust matters.
- The store claims clarity, support, and better curation — not magic.

- Primary: `#123C2F`

- Gold accent: `#C8972C`
- Sand background: `#F7F1E8`
- Ink: `#171412`

Logo idea:

- Right side: circular monogram with Arabic letters **س ش** inside primary green circle.
- Next to it: text logo **سوق الشام**, below smaller **souksham**.
- Keep text logo clean and premium.
